Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

STED is made by PC Labs, a small independent developer. This page explains what information STED works with and how it is handled. We wrote it in plain language on purpose, because a privacy policy should not need a law degree to understand.

Who we are

PC Labs built STED as a simple, private streak tracker. You can reach us anytime at pclabs.contact@gmail.com.

What STED does

STED helps you track how long it has been since you last did, or avoided, something you care about. It works entirely on your own device. There is no account to create and no server behind it.

What information STED stores

When you set up STED, you are asked for your name. This is required, since it is used to personalize the app for you, for example greeting you by name. Beyond that, STED stores whatever you choose to enter as you use the app:

  • The streaks you create, including their names, categories, icons, and dates
  • Your check-in and streak history, including resets, pauses, and archived streaks
  • App settings you choose, like your theme, date format, and notification preferences

All of this is entered by you and stored on your device. We do not ask for anything else, and we do not collect it automatically.

Why this information is used

Everything STED stores exists for one reason, to make the app work the way you would expect. Your name is used to greet you. Your streak data is used to calculate and display your streaks. Your settings control how the app looks and behaves. None of it is used for anything beyond running the app on your device.

How your information is stored

STED stores your data locally on your device, using a standard local database and Android's app storage system. There is no cloud database and no account tied to your information.

Does STED send anything to a server?

No. STED does not operate its own servers, and it does not send your name, your streaks, or any other information you enter to us or to anyone else. Everything happens on your device.

That said, if your Android device has its own backup turned on (the kind built into Android, tied to your Google account), the operating system may include STED's app data as part of that backup, the same way it does for most other apps on your phone. That is a feature of your device and your Google account, not something STED does on its own. STED itself never uploads anything.

Does STED share information with anyone else?

No. STED does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and it does not include any third-party services that would receive your data. There is simply nowhere for your information to go outside your device.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders or milestone notifications, STED schedules and shows them using Android's own notification system, entirely on your device. Nothing about your notifications, including their content or timing, is sent anywhere else. You can turn notifications on or off anytime in Settings, or through your device's notification settings.

Backup and restore

STED lets you export your data to a file whenever you want, using your device's own file picker. This file includes your streaks, your check-in history, and your app settings, including your name. You choose where it is saved, whether that is your device storage or a cloud storage app you use yourself. STED does not send this file anywhere on its own. If you later import a backup file, STED reads it and replaces your current data with what is in the file. Both exporting and importing only happen when you choose to do them.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep the app installed, or until you delete it yourself. You can delete individual streaks at any time, or clear everything at once using the Reset Application Data option in Settings. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage through your device settings, also removes everything STED has stored. Since we never had a copy of your data to begin with, there is nothing on our end to delete.

Security

Because your data never leaves your device, its security depends mainly on your device's own security, like your screen lock and Android's built-in app sandboxing. We recommend keeping your device secured the way you normally would.

Children's privacy

STED does not have a way to verify a user's age, and it does not need to, since it does not collect anything beyond what you type in yourself, and that never leaves your device. If you are a parent and have concerns about a child using STED, you can review or remove all stored data at any time through the app or your device settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and make the new version available in the app. Using STED after an update means you are okay with the changes.

Questions
